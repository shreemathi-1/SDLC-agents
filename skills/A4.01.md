# A4.01 ADR writing

- Agent: A4 Architect
- Skill: ADR writing
- Phase: 1
- Human gate: No

## Purpose
Creates architecture decision records (context, options, decision, consequences)
(Source: SDLC_Agent_Skills_Excel.xlsx, via input/phase1_skill_details.csv)

## Steps (PROPOSED, human approves; R10)
1. Write the context.
2. List at least two options.
3. Record the decision as proposed; a human records the final one with a date.

## Verification (PROPOSED)
Each ADR has at least two options and consequences.
Rules [R1-R10](../rules/R1-R10.md) also apply.
