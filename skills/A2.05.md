# A2.05 Definition of Ready check

- Agent: A2 Requirements
- Skill: Definition of Ready check
- Phase: 1
- Human gate: Yes (an agent never marks it done; see R5)

## Purpose
Verifies a story meets the team's Definition of Ready
(Source: SDLC_Agent_Skills_Excel.xlsx, via input/phase1_skill_details.csv)

## Steps (PROPOSED, human approves; R10)
1. Check each story against the team's Definition of Ready.
2. Record pass or fail per item with evidence.
3. Send Ready to the Product Owner for approval.

## Verification (PROPOSED)
Every item has pass/fail evidence. Definition of Ready (PROPOSED, not yet approved by Product Owner; open_questions_answers.md item 5). Ready when:
1. Role, goal, benefit stated. 2. At least one Given/When/Then criterion with one observable result each.
3. Platforms stated or logged TBD with an open question. 4. Every open question answered by a named person or accepted by the Product Owner.
5. No criterion depends on an unresolved TBD. 6. Source ID cited.
Rules [R1-R10](../rules/R1-R10.md) also apply.
